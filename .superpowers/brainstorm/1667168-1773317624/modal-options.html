How should the video detail modal look?

When you click a video tile, what layout should the expanded view use?

A: Centered Modal

Classic modal overlay. Video player on top, title and description below. Dimmed background. Click outside or press Escape to close.

B: Slide-in Side Panel

Panel slides in from the right. Grid stays visible (dimmed) on the left. Video player and details in the panel. Good for quick browsing without losing context.

C: Full-Page Detail

Replaces the grid entirely. Large video player on the left, metadata on the right. "Back to feed" link to return. Most immersive viewing experience.
